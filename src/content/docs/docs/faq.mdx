## Some things you may be wondering

TLH - The Language Hub

<details>
    <summary>Who is The Language Hub for?</summary>

    Anyone and everyone interested in langauges, and the way people communicate.

</details>
<details>
    <summary>What kind of contributions does TLH look for?</summary>

    We need help in two areas:
    1. The language content, ideally from speakers from all across the globe.
    2. Imporvements on the infrastucture holding TLH together.

</details>
<details>
    <summary>Do I need to be a developer to contribute?</summary>

    Absolutely not!
    Anyone who is willing to share what they know is more than welcome to add to TLH.

</details>
<details>
<summary>How can I start adding to TLH</summary>
You can have a look at our contribution guides for what you're inclined to:

1. [Add Langugage Data](/docs/contributing/language)
2. [Add or Correct Code](/docs/contributing/code)

</details>
